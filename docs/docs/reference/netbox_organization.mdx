---
title: NetBox Organization
---

Regions, site groups, sites, locations, tenants, tenant groups, contacts, contact groups, contact roles and contact assignments: the Organization section of the NetBox navigation menu, mapped from the NetBox 4.7 dcim and tenancy tables. Load it first: it also defines the generics that the other NetBox schemas use for descriptions, comments, tags, tenants and contacts.

## Details

- **Dependencies:** No dependencies

## Use cases

This extension covers:
- Import the NetBox sites with their regions, site groups and locations, and list every region, site group or location below another one, through the Infrahub hierarchy of each tree.
- Record which customer or department each site, device, prefix, circuit or tunnel belongs to.
- Assign contacts, with a role and a priority, to the objects that accept contacts in NetBox.

Out of scope, and what to reach for instead:
- NetBox owners and owner groups (users app). Use Infrahub accounts, groups and permissions.
- NetBox custom fields. Add attributes to the schema instead.
- Regions, site groups, tenants and contact groups with the same name under different parents. Their names are unique in Infrahub, so that other objects can refer to them by name.
- Unique location names. NetBox makes them unique within a site and an optional parent location, which an Infrahub uniqueness constraint cannot include.

## Nodes

### Region

- **Description:** Arranges sites geographically, for example by continent, country, city or campus. Regions can be nested.
- **Namespace:** Netbox
- **Icon:** mdi:earth
- **Human Friendly ID:** name__value
- **Inherit From:** NetboxGeneric, NetboxContactAssignable, NetboxPrefixScope, NetboxVLANGroupScope, NetboxCircuitTerminationPoint, NetboxRegionHierarchy

- **Description:** Region, site group, site or location that can scope a prefix. netbox/netbox_ipam adds the relationship.

- **Description:** Region, site group, site, location, rack group or rack that can scope a VLAN group. Completed by netbox_ipam.
