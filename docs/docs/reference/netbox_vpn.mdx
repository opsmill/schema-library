---
title: NetBox VPN
---

Tunnels, tunnel groups, tunnel terminations, IKE and IPSec proposals, policies and profiles, and L2VPNs with their terminations, mapped from the NetBox 4.7 vpn tables.

## Details

- **Dependencies:**
    - [netbox/netbox_organization](netbox_organization)
    - [netbox/netbox_devices](netbox_devices)
    - [netbox/netbox_ipam](netbox_ipam)

## Use cases

This extension covers:
- Import NetBox tunnels with their IPSec profiles, the device interfaces they terminate on, and the outside IP address of each termination.
- Import L2VPNs, such as VXLAN-EVPN or VPLS, with their route targets and the interfaces or VLANs they terminate on.

Out of scope, and what to reach for instead:
- VM interfaces (virtualization) as tunnel or L2VPN terminations.
