---
title: NetBox Circuits
---

Providers, provider accounts, provider networks, circuit types, circuits, circuit terminations, circuit groups and virtual circuits, mapped from the NetBox 4.7 circuits tables. A circuit termination ends on a region, site group, site, location or provider network, and a cable connects it to a device interface or port.

## Details

- **Dependencies:**
    - [netbox/netbox_organization](netbox_organization)
    - [netbox/netbox_devices](netbox_devices)

## Use cases

This extension covers:
- Import NetBox circuits with their A and Z terminations, the site or provider network each termination ends on, and the cable to the device port.
- Record virtual circuits between device interfaces over a provider network.

Out of scope, and what to reach for instead:
- Provider ASNs when netbox/netbox_ipam is not loaded. Load netbox/netbox_ipam, in any order, to add the ASNs relationship to providers.
